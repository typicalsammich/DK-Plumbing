# DK Plumbing & Heating Backlink and Citation Plan

This plan prioritizes legitimate local and trade relevance. It avoids bulk link packages, paid blog networks and copied directory profiles.

## 1. Lock down business identity

Use the same business name, phone, email, service-area wording and website URL everywhere:

- DK Plumbing & Heating LLC
- 720-527-4557
- dkplumbingandheating@icloud.com
- Colorado Master Plumber PC.0005620
- Denver Metro, Colorado Springs and surrounding Front Range communities

Confirm whether the Cobblecreek Drive address should be public or hidden as a service-area business before expanding citations.

## 2. Primary profiles

Complete, verify and keep current:

1. Google Business Profile
2. Bing Places
3. Apple Business Connect
4. Facebook
5. Instagram
6. Yelp
7. Nextdoor Business Page
8. BuildZoom
9. BBB, if membership and complaint-handling requirements make sense for DK

Use real project photos, the circular DK logo, current service descriptions and the same contact information on each profile.

## 3. Colorado and trade citations

Pursue listings only where DK is eligible and the directory is actively maintained:

- Local chambers of commerce in the core service area
- Colorado contractor and home-service directories
- Plumbing and hydronic industry associations
- Boiler, water-heater and plumbing-supply manufacturer contractor directories
- Local builder and remodeler associations
- Commercial property-management vendor directories

Do not create profiles in cities DK does not actually serve.

## 4. Contractor relationship links

DK's contractor relationships are the strongest natural link opportunity.

Ask regular general contractors, deck builders, remodelers and homebuilders to credit DK on project pages where DK performed the plumbing. A useful credit is specific and editorial, for example:

> Plumbing and gas piping by DK Plumbing & Heating LLC

The link should point to the most relevant DK page, such as Commercial Plumbing, Contractor Support, Gas Line Services or Projects.

## 5. Project-led outreach

For each substantial finished project:

1. Publish a short DK project entry using real images and factual scope.
2. Ask the general contractor or project partner to link to it from their project page.
3. Tag the equipment manufacturer or supplier when appropriate.
4. Share the same project through Google Business Profile, Facebook and Instagram.

Never invent an address, budget, client name or project result that is not approved for publication.

## 6. Community authority

Support local organizations only when the relationship is genuine:

- Youth sports or neighborhood events
- Local charities
- Trade-school programs
- Community repair days
- Builder or property-manager education events

When the organization lists sponsors or partners, request the correct DK name and website link.

## 7. Review and referral loop

After completed work, ask customers for a Google review and permission to photograph the final installation. Do not offer compensation in exchange for positive sentiment. Encourage honest, specific feedback about communication, cleanliness and the completed service.

## 8. Monthly tracking

Track these items once per month:

- New referring domains
- Incorrect business citations
- Links to broken or redirected pages
- Google Business Profile views and calls
- Organic traffic to service and city pages
- Estimate requests by landing page

Prioritize a small number of relevant links and complete profiles over raw backlink volume.
